## About

Miyaan AI is a Bengali-first initiative dedicated to making Artificial Intelligence easier to understand, learn, and use. We explore practical AI technologies, create useful resources, and build open-source projects that turn AI knowledge into real-world skills.

> Our goal is simple: language should never be a barrier to learning AI and benefiting from it.

We bring together AI education, technology exploration, hands-on experimentation, and community-driven innovation. Everyone deserves the chance to understand and benefit from artificial intelligence, whatever their technical background or preferred language.

## Why Miyaan AI?

Artificial Intelligence is transforming education, employment, business, and everyday life. But access to quality AI knowledge is not equal.

Language barriers, technical complexity, and a shortage of practical learning resources keep many people from joining this transformation.

We want to change that.

> **The Bengali-speaking generation should not just consume AI technology. It should learn, build, innovate, and contribute to it.**

---

## Mission and Vision

**Our Mission**

To make AI knowledge accessible, practical, and useful for everyone in the Bengali-speaking world through education, open-source technology, and real-world experimentation.

**Our Vision**

A future where language is no barrier to technological opportunity, and the Bengali-speaking generation actively participates in building the AI-powered world.
